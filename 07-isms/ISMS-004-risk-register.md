# Information Security Risk Register

## Purpose & Scope

This register holds every information security risk identified inside the scope of [ISMS-001](ISMS-001-scope-and-context.md), rated on the scales of [ISMS-003](ISMS-003-risk-methodology.md). Each row names the asset or process, the threat and the weakness at a level that is safe to publish, the controls already in place, the risk owner role, the rating now and at production, and the decision. The treatment actions and the Statement of Applicability are in [ISMS-005](ISMS-005-risk-treatment-and-soa.md).

Today only the development environment runs. Staging, production and the Notification Platform are designed, not built. Every risk is therefore rated twice ([ISMS-003 §Scales](ISMS-003-risk-methodology.md#scales)): **now**, for the development environment, and **at production**, if the same weakness were carried into production. A row marked "not carried" describes a weakness that exists only in development and that the production design does not repeat.

## Record

### This assessment

- **Date and basis:** 1 November 2026. The first full assessment. It starts from an evidence review of 31 October 2026 across the eight Scnehaux repositories, updated for the changes merged since (the image-scan fixes in identity-kernel and the neutral host names).
- **Method:** [ISMS-003](ISMS-003-risk-methodology.md), unchanged.
- **Not verified in this assessment:** GitHub repository settings other than organization-control's ruleset; the development host's time source, disk encryption, patching and list of root holders; whether the Keycloak master administrator has a second factor; the host's physical site. Each appears below as a weakness or a treatment action, not as a working control.
- **Rating notation:** likelihood × impact = score and level. For example `3 × 4 = 12 High`.

- **No risk is High now.** Every High rating is at production. Under [ISMS-003 §Acceptance criteria](ISMS-003-risk-methodology.md#acceptance-criteria) each of the 15 has a treatment that is a production gate (ISMS-005).
- **Medium risks** stay in place until their treatment completes. Those retained in development by decision (R1, R4, R5, R10, R11) need the risk owner's acceptance with the ISMS Owner's agreement, reviewed by **30 April 2027** at the latest. Approving this record gives that acceptance.
- **The two nearest dates** are R8 (16 November 2026) and R22 (30 November 2026). If either passes without action, the daily scans on the affected repositories fail.

## Review & Change Control

- **Reviewer and approver:** the Information Security Officer reviews; the ISMS Owner approves ([GDC-012 §2.3.2](../00-governance/GDC-012-isms-guideline.md#232-taxonomy)).
- **Cadence:** every 90 days at most. The next full review is due by 30 January 2027.
- **Early review when:** a new environment, service or supplier is used; a standard or decision a control relies on changes; an image-scan exception is taken or reaches its review date; after an incident, a nonconformity or an audit finding.
- Each change is a pull request, so who rated what, and when, is in Git history.
