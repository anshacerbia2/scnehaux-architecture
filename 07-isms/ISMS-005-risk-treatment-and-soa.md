# Risk Treatment Plan and Statement of Applicability

## Purpose & Scope

This record has two parts, both inside the scope of [ISMS-001](ISMS-001-scope-and-context.md):

1. **The treatment plan:** one action per treatment chosen in the risk register ([ISMS-004](ISMS-004-risk-register.md)), and one per missing part of a control, each with an owner role, a target date and the residual risk expected.
2. **The Statement of Applicability (SoA):** all 93 controls of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A, whether each applies and why, its control status ([GDC-012 §2.3.6](../00-governance/GDC-012-isms-guideline.md#236-control-status-vocabulary)) and its evidence.

A control is judged over the Scnehaux services, their data and the development environment, which is all that runs today. A control is `implemented` only where a document sets the rule **and** a script, CI job or the code enforces it, with a link to that enforcement. Evidence links point at the `main` branch of each repository.

### Residual risk acceptance

Approving this record gives these acceptances, each within the limits of [ISMS-003 §Acceptance criteria](ISMS-003-risk-methodology.md#acceptance-criteria):

- **Development risks retained** (R1, R4, R5, R10, R11, all Medium now): accepted by their risk owners with the ISMS Owner's agreement, until 30 April 2027 or until the treatment completes, whichever is first.
- **Low risks retained** (R9, R20, R22): accepted by their risk owners until the register's next review.
- **Expected residuals at production** are Medium or lower for every risk. They are accepted only when each action is complete and its result is checked; an action that leaves a High residual returns to Top Management.

## Review & Change Control

- **Reviewer and approver:** the Information Security Officer reviews; the ISMS Owner approves ([GDC-012 §2.3.2](../00-governance/GDC-012-isms-guideline.md#232-taxonomy)). Each risk owner role named in ISMS-004 approves the acceptance of its residual risk.
- **Cadence:** every 90 days at most, together with the risk register. The next review is due by 30 January 2027.
- **Early review when:** an action's target date passes; an action completes; a control's evidence moves or breaks; a new risk enters the register; after an incident, a nonconformity or an audit finding.
